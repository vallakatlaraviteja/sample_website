About TerraPulse

A live 3D map of new AI/ML repositories on GitHub, geo-located to their authoring organisation.

  • AI launches — repos created in the last 7 days with at least 30 stars matching AI/ML keywords. Pulled live from the GitHub Search API.
  • Quakes — last-24h feed from USGS.
  • ISS — current location of the International Space Station via wheretheiss.at, refreshed every 5 seconds.

Org coordinates are a curated list of ~40 major AI labs. Repos from un-mapped owners are not shown — by design, no fabricated coordinates.

This is the static demo. The full version (sign-in with GitHub, moderated community pulses, daily email alerts, Stripe-backed Verified tier) is in the same repo and deploys to Render via render.yaml.
